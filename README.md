## Hi, I'm Dastan 👋

I'm a founder and software engineer who likes turning complex systems into products people can actually use. My work spans predictive analytics, consumer apps, and payroll integrations. I care about clear interfaces, dependable infrastructure, and showing the evidence behind a product's claims.

## Building now

### 01 / Seer9 — prediction intelligence

I'm building [Seer9](https://seer9.com), a research terminal for people who want to make more informed decisions in sports prediction markets. It combines quantitative forecasts, market-price comparisons, and live context in one place. The product publishes its [methodology](https://seer9.com/methodology) and [settled model results](https://seer9.com/proof) so people can inspect how the estimates perform.

**Explore:** [Live product](https://seer9.com) · [Public results](https://seer9.com/proof) · [Methodology](https://seer9.com/methodology)

### 02 / NeuroHack — daily cognitive practice

I built and released [NeuroHack](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/neurohack/id6754859121) for iPhone and iPad. It delivers short, voice-guided exercises aimed at patterns such as overthinking, procrastination, and negative self-talk. The goal is to make a small daily practice easy to start and repeat.

**Explore:** [NeuroHack on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/neurohack/id6754859121)
